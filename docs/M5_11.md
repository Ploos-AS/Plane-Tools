# M5.11 — ADS-B receiver health states

Plane Tools now exposes one receiver-health model through both `/api/v1/adsb/status` and `/api/v1/adsb/snapshot`.

## States

- `healthy`: receiver fetch succeeds and feed timestamp is at most 15 seconds old.
- `degraded`: receiver fetch succeeds but feed age is over 15 and at most 60 seconds, or a fetch fails within 60 seconds of the last successful fetch.
- `stale`: feed timestamp is over 60 seconds old, or failures continue for more than 60 seconds after the last success.
- `offline`: receiver is not configured, or a fetch fails before any successful fetch has been observed.

Status responses include `health`, `health_reason`, and when applicable `feed_age_seconds` or `last_success_age_seconds`.

The web UI uses the same health field and renders healthy, degraded, stale and offline states consistently. Exact receiver coordinates are no longer printed in the live status line; it only indicates whether receiver position is configured.

No external telemetry, provider calls, map services or persistent health-history storage are introduced by this milestone.
